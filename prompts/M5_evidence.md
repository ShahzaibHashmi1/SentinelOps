RANKING (evidence/app/ranking.py, pure logic; weights in ranking.json)
Components, each with a cap and a plain-language reason: anomaly strength (how far above its threshold the worst signal is), precedence (earlier first_anomalous_at than the other anomalous services), hard container evidence (container missing or down, OOM, crash loop), recent change (a change record of kind recreated or config_changed in the 120 s before the first anomaly of that service), origin position (anomalous while every dependency in the graph is healthy), shared dependency (two or more anomalous services depend on it), effect demotion (an anomalous service with an anomalous dependency is demoted: its symptom is explained by that dependency), log evidence (errors and dependency errors that name the service), detector hint (primary_service gets a small bonus; candidate_services a smaller one). Scores are clipped to 0 to 100; ties are broken by service name. no_single_root is true when the detector hint has a symptom with role "load", or when the top two scores are within the margin in ranking.json.
